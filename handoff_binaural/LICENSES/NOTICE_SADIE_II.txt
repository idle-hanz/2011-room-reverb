Changes (Apache-2.0 s.4(b)): derived by the 2011 Room Reverb project from D1/D2 HRIR SOFA files
(D*_44K_16bit_256tap_FIR_SOFA.sofa, D*_48K_24bit_256tap_FIR_SOFA.sofa): diffuse-field level normalisation,
1/6-octave smoothing, left/right mirror averaging at 60 grid nodes, RBJ biquad fit per ear and rate,
ITD analysis. No raw HRIRs are redistributed.
