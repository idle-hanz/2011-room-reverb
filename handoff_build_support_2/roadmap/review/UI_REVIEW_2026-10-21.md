## G. Space-lattice room-mic positions — CONFIRMED (2026-10-21 follow-up)

**Verdict: confirmed.** The Space tab lattice does **not** use room-mic positions. Play and Render do.

Trace:
1. Play/Render: `heardMix` (app.js:597-605) writes `mic_x_*/mic_y_*/mic_z_*` from each capsule's room spot when `place_*=="room"` (or centre third is Room). `/api/live_params` and `/api/render` take that body. `render_pipeline._place_stand` → `desk_matrix.place_stand` / `position_override` (engine/desk_matrix.py:329-341) moves each capsule to those coordinates.
2. Space lattice: `fetchImages` (app.js:1461) posts **raw `state`**, not `heardMix`, so even the client never converts `room_l_x` into `mic_x_l` for this call. `api_images` (app_server.py:918-927) calls `build_images_payload` with only the stand knobs (`mcx`, `mcz`, `my`, `space`, …). The function signature (image_geometry.py:168-194) has **no** `mic_x_*` parameters.
3. Probe on Idle's PC (2026-10-21), room 6×3×8, stand at (3, 1.5, 1.5), non-default room mics L(0.5,1.8,4) C(3,2,7) R(5.5,1.8,4):
   - Lattice capsules: L(2.915,1.5,1.5) C(3,1.5,1.5) R(3.085,1.5,1.5) — the ORTF stand.
   - Play/Render after `place_stand`: L(0.5,1.8,4) C(3,2,7) R(5.5,1.8,4).
   - Error: L 3.489 m, C 5.523 m, R 3.489 m.
4. Effect for the user: with any capsule on "Room mic", the Space lattice / Paths / Last legs still draw the stand array. Paths, delays and levels shown there are wrong for those capsules. Floor/Mic editors show the true spots.
5. Severity: **Med**. Effort: **small** (pass `mic_x_*` into `build_images_payload` / use `_place_stand`, and have `fetchImages` send `heardMix(state)`). UI/visual only; the sound path is already correct. No SOUND flag.

## LIVE WALKTHROUGH (2026-10-21, real app on the box)

How it ran: this is a copy of `software\` on the box (your tree was not touched). It used uvicorn on :8765 and headless Chrome at 1280x800 and 1600x1000, with test_pluck.wav (mono, 4 s). It made 29 screenshots, which are on the box at `/workspace/rr_live_shots/`.
Not run: the full mic_fitter (files over 8 MB were skipped), the handoff_binaural and source_radiation packs, and the Windows DLL (Python render worked). WebGL was software-rendered and not pixel-checked. The audio was not listened to.
Console: no JS errors and no failed requests. There were two warnings: the three.min.js deprecation and getImageData willReadFrequently. **A first run without mic_fitter/ beside software/ gave HTTP 500 on most APIs.** The app depends on the repo layout, and the UI gives no plain message when it is missing.

### Per tab
- **Room** works. Plan, 3D and side view draw. At 1280 the floor-plan labels are 6–7 px. The sidebar is Room size, Preview and Advanced; there is **no mic setup here**.
- **Source** works. At 1280 the radiation polar is only about 140 px and its ring labels are unreadable. The size chip and Advanced drop below the 3-column grid.
- **Mic** works. All four graphs draw. At 1280 the page is very tall (Source plane sits about 1.5 screens down).
- **Mix** works. Play live ran ("Live FDN running") and the faders, mutes and Decay respond. The text is very small (see U36).
- **Space** works. The lattice draws, and Paths and Last legs switch. The Hadamard panel lives only here.
- **Surfaces** works. Room result, Room colour and the absorption graphs all draw. Room colour fills after Render.
- **Polar**: all three modes draw (Combined, Each mic, At speakers).
- **Render**: about 32 s for 4 s of audio. The status shows FDN 2.79 s and T60 1.80 s, and out.wav plays in Preview.
