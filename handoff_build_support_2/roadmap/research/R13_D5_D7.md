## D7: small room decays short (N10)
**Measured on the box (Render path, harness `render_ir`, `proto/d7_render.py`/`.json`):** scene small_lock08 = 4 × 2.5 × 5 m, dial 0.8 s.

| band | 250 | 500 | 1 k | 2 k | 4 k | 8 k | broadband |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T30 (s) | 0.703 | 0.753 | 0.768 | 0.745 | 0.773 | 0.785 | 0.774 |
| vs 0.8 s | −12 % | −5.9 % | −4.0 % | −6.9 % | −3.4 % | −1.9 % | −3.3 % |

- For comparison, std_lock18 (dial 1.8) reads 250 Hz −9.8 %, then 500 Hz to 2 kHz within ±5 %.
- **Play could not be measured on the box today:** the 2 Oct harness `wasm_run.js` does not match the build-51 blob and returns silence for every scene. The last recorded Play figure is 1 kHz 0.732 s (−8.5 %). So Render is now within the 5 % JND at 1 kHz, but Play was not.
- JND for decay time is about 5 % (ISO 3382-1 Annex A; **ASSUMED**, not re-read). The low-band shortfall is in both rooms, so it looks like the bass decay handling rather than a small-room effect.

**Recommendation:** first re-measure Play on the PC (the existing `--slow` gate). If Play is still about −8 %, it is a Play-vs-Render gap: treat it as a bug in the live tank (M). If Play now matches Render (about −4 %), accept it and widen the small-room tolerance to ±10 % at 250 Hz only.
**Decision:** **D7**, accept/widen vs fix, after the PC re-measure.
