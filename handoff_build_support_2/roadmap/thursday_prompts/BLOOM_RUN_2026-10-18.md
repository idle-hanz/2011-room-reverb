## Why (plain language)
A room mic through a compressor sounds bigger because the compressor turns the room down while you play and lets it swell back up between notes. Idle wants that musical effect, but done cleanly inside the reverb: no gain on the whole signal, so no extra noise, rumble or clipping. Bloom is a level follower that listens to the dry input and steers only how loud the reverb tail is. The direct sound and the early reflections are never touched, so attacks keep their character. The presets borrow the timing and curve shapes of real compressors (LA-2A, SSL bus, 1176, Distressor, Fairchild) from the circuit and SPICE research. Predelay holds the tail back a little after the dry sound, like a plate or hall predelay.

## Hard rules (D-BLOOM, binding)
1. **Tail only.** A follower on the DRY input steers only the tail: its input level (what reaches the FDN inject) and/or its return level (how loud the tank output comes back). Everything happens **before the limiter** (`soft_limit` in `mix_partition`, `live_stereo.c` ~3428-3444; `apply_soft_limit` in `render_pipeline.py` 434, called at ~2064 / ~2269 / ~2408). No gain after the limiter.
2. **Direct and early untouched.** No change to direct (`dir` ring, `daBlk`/`dbBlk`), the early lanes (`LANE_EARLY`, `eaBlk`/`ebBlk`/`esBlk`), the dry (`dryBlk`) or their FIR/colour paths. Any move in direct or early bits with Bloom on is a STOP.
3. **Tank unchanged.** The Puckette/Hadamard tank, the unscaled Hadamard mix, the 1/sqrt(N) decay-lock loop, `g_decay`, line absorption, Surfaces cascade, ROOMTONE harmonics, `sub_hp`, `phys_ceiling` are not edited. Bloom steers what goes in and how loud it comes out. No coefficient inside the loop changes (Sustain is NOT this run).
4. **Off is bit-exact, proven first.** Amount 0 and Predelay 0 (the defaults): the five goldens, GOLDEN_META, both live MD5s and the d18/d4 prefixes are byte-identical to the Current pin. The live dict grows no key while off (the MD5s hash the whole dict). The new blob sections are absent while off, and the C off path never touches Bloom state (same pattern as `subHpOn`, `live_stereo.c` 1702 / 1792-1802 / 2492-2508). This proof runs before any Bloom-on work. Any move is a STOP.
5. **Play and Render match.** One shared parameter function (Python) feeds `live_fdn_params` and every Render branch. The envelope maths is the same in Python and C, in double, with the same order of operations, the same coefficients (designed once in Python, packed, never re-derived in the worklet) and the same freestanding math (`ls_exp` already exists at `live_stereo.c` 481 and `render_pipeline.py` 391; add an `ls_log` the same way if a log is needed, mirrored exactly). 7. **No analogue-style makeup.** Bloom does not apply a whole-signal makeup gain the way a compressor does. Ambience grows only by tail steering.
