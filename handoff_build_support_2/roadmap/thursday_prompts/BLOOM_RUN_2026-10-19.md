## Why (plain language)
A room mic through a compressor sounds bigger. The compressor turns the room down while you play, then lets it swell back up between notes. Idle wants that effect done cleanly inside the reverb. **Bloom** is a level follower that listens to the dry input and steers only how loud the reverb tail comes back. Direct and early sound are never touched. The presets copy real compressors: LA-2A, SSL bus, 1176, Distressor and Fairchild.

On 19 Oct, a second round of measurements against Idle's UAD plug-ins showed three things. First, four presets were too slow, Room Crush, Nuke and Swell above all. Second, real units add a little fixed harmonic colour and a fixed EQ, and the colour does not come from the compressing itself. Third, Volterra or "dynamic convolution" models lose badly to a compressor model with a small colour block (round 2 section 4). Idle listened to the A/B clips. He heard the UAD 610-A and Neve pres saturate more than our clean versions, and a big difference on one Nuke clip. The rest sounded clean. **Idle decided (2026-10-19 18:04 BST, binding):** apply every proposed timing fix, add the hybrid colour stage, and add a mic-pre colour stage for the 610-A and the Neve.

### C. Mic-pre colour stage (610-A and Neve 1073)
