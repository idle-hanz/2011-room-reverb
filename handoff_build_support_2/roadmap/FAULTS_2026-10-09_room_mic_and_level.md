## F1 - Level goes the wrong way with distance; limiter fires when the mic is FAR (HIGH)
Idle's words: "I like the automatic gain control but it seems to work too well. The sound seems to get softer as it approaches the mic." Later: "I tried it with the early reflections and the behaviour was the same. I even got the limiter to kick in when the sound was far away. It might also explain the loud sounds when you press the hall button."
Evidence (VERIFIED, screenshots): Screenshot_554.png - Play live, Omni source, room mic C placed far from the source (bottom right of the floor plan): the header shows "Limiter: peak reduced by 14.1 dB (582 samples)". Screenshot_555.png - same scene, the room mic C placed right on the source: no limiter badge. So the loud peaks happen FAR away and the level falls as the mic gets CLOSE, the opposite of a real room (direct sound should get louder as the mic nears; far should be quieter).
Also reported: the Hall button is very loud. A big room means long source-to-mic distances, which fits the same fault.
Hypothesis (ASSUMED, not tested): the Play/Render "makeup" gain (the automatic gain, "running Mixer level from inject energy" in Play, ir_rms_makeup_from_inject in Render; render_pipeline.py ~1310, ~1611, ~2205; live_fdn.js:380 / pack_room.js:47; live_stereo.c) scales roughly with 1 / (inject energy). Inject energy falls with distance, so the makeup rises with distance: far = hot, near = quiet. That would also push the soft limiter (D-AUD-3) when far, and make Hall (long distances) loud. It would hold the tail at a fixed level independent of distance, but it appears to over-correct.
To check (read-only measurement, no change to the sound): sweep source-to-mic distance (about 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 m, plus Studio, Club and Hall presets) with the Direct and Early faders both down and both up. For each point record the makeup value posted to Play, the Render makeup, the FDN tail RMS and the direct and early levels in dB, the peak, and the limiter reduction; compare Play with Render. State plainly which quantity moves the wrong way and where. Related decisions already open: N2 S-OUT Auto level (never answered), N4 master headroom, D-AUD-3 limiter. Do NOT change anything: report numbers; Idle decides.
