# F9 diagnosis: "physics path decays 20 % long" (2026-10-19)

Read-only. No file under `software\` or the tests was edited. Scratch scripts and IRs are in `%TEMP%\f9_diag\` (`common.py`, `synth.py`, `synth6.py`, `run1.py`, `run2.py`, `fast.py`, `guard.py`, `results.jsonl`, `ir_*.npz`).

## In plain words

**The reverb is not 20 % long. The ruler was bent.** The +20 % (and the -12 % at 125 Hz) comes from how the AMBIENCE and SMALLDECAY listen scripts measured the decay, not from the engine.

Those scripts took the two IR channels and turned them into one "loudness" wave (`np.hypot(L, R)`, always positive), *then* split that into octave bands to time the decay. Making a wave all-positive scrambles its frequencies: the sound at 2 kHz in the new wave is a mix of beats between every band, so each band's measured decay gets dragged towards the overall average. Bands that really decay fast (2-4 kHz) read too long. Bands that really decay slowly (125 Hz) read too short. That is exactly the F9 pattern.

Proof, in three steps:
1. **Fake reverb with known decay times.** I made noise that decays at exactly the model row in every octave and measured it both ways. The old method reported +10 / +24 / +31 / +27 % at 500 / 1k / 2k / 4k and -3 % at 125 Hz, on a signal that is perfect by construction. The correct method (split each channel into bands first, then add their energies) read within about ±4 %.
2. **The real boot IR, measured correctly.** Same render as `boot_6x3x8_B_phys`. From 125 Hz to 2 kHz it lands within ±1 % of the target. 4 kHz is +6 %, and 63 Hz is -10 %.
3. **The engine's own design numbers agree.** Adding up each delay line's designed loss per trip, weighted by delay (how the Hadamard tank averages it), predicts the same decay the correct measurement finds, within 1-3 %.

So at dial 1.8 the boot mids really are about 1.99 s at 500 Hz and 1.57 s at 1 kHz (mean 1.78 s against the 1.80 target). The designed bass lift really lands: T(125) / mid is **1.30**, as designed, not the "about 1×" the old ruler showed. What you heard in B, C and D is the decay the model asked for.

**Does it need fixing?** The sound doesn't, at dial 1.8. The measuring script and the notes do. There are also three smaller *real* errors, unrelated to the 20 %. They are listed below and are optional.

## Root causes, with confidence

1. **F9 as logged (+20 % from 500 Hz up, −12 % at 125 Hz) is a measurement artefact.** The cause is `np.hypot(L, R)` before octave filtering in `roadmap\ambience\make_listens.py::octave_t60` (and the SMALLDECAY listens, whose report note says "T20 on the stereo magnitude"). **Confidence: very high.** The synthetic signal reproduces the pattern, the proper ruler on the same IR is within ±1 % from 125 Hz to 2 kHz, and the design arithmetic agrees. It is not a section-fit bias. The T60-to-loss conversion uses each line's own delay (`tau = d/sr` per line, the right choice). Air is counted once (in `room_target`; the inject air LP is outside the loop). Hadamard mixing averages the lines as expected. The old pins and notes built on it are wrong: the HANDOVER boot IR line, the SMALLDECAY bass row 2.08 / 2.34 / 2.16, "the 1.2-1.4× ratio lands ~1×", and possibly F12's numbers if they were measured the same way.
2. **Real, smaller (A): ROOMTONE harmonic cuts are not in the fit.** `harmonic_stages` biquads run in the loop after the sections, but `design_bus` folds in only `lg` and the kept axial peak (`fixed`). In rooms where the size gate turns ROOMTONE on (3×4×2.5, 2×2.5×2.2), 125-500 Hz lands 12-20 % short of T_eff. Turning ROOMTONE off in the ablation puts 3×4×2.5 back within +4 %. The dial path has the same extra loss by design. **Confidence: high.**
3. **Real, smaller (B): at dials 4 and 8 a few lines' fits go above unity at DC.** The guard then reverts them to identity, i.e. dial-flat. This gives 4 kHz +10 % (dial 4) and +15 % (dial 8), and 500 Hz −12 % at dial 8, where T_eff(500) = 9.09 s is above the 8 s dial. **Likely mechanism (medium confidence):** with `lg` already at the dial, the loss margin per trip is tiny (about −0.07 dB per 20 ms at 8 s). The low shelf that compensates the kept sub-30 Hz axial cut and the 63 Hz clamp overshoots at DC. The penalty in `resid` is too soft to stop it.
4. **Real, minor (C): 63 Hz runs short (−10 % boot, −27 % in 4.5×6, worse in small rooms).** Phys flat keeps every first-axial cut (`keep_cut_below_hz = 1e9`), and the S1P2 sections can't fill a narrow notch. The dial path is short there too (−28 %). The 63 Hz band in these rooms holds only a few modes and is the noisiest measurement. **Confidence: medium-high.**

## Fix options

**Option 1. Fix the ruler and the notes (recommended now).**
- **What changes:** in the listen scripts, `octave_t60` filters L and R separately and sums the band energies, or uses `ir.sum(1)` like the tests. Re-measure the AMBIENCE and SMALLDECAY report rows. Correct the HANDOVER boot IR line and F9 (close it as a measurement artefact). Re-check F12 with a linear narrow-band method.
- **Accuracy:** shows the true ±1 % (125 Hz-2 kHz) at dial 1.8.
- **Sound:** none. **Pins, CPU and Hadamard:** none. No wasm, golden, MD5 or `WORKLET_VER` change. **Play and Render:** neither (they already null at −78 dB).
- **Physics default:** this removes F9 as a blocker. For question 9: with physics as the default, t10 / t12 (±15 % of the dial, 500 Hz-8 kHz) will fail at 2-8 kHz because the physics target itself falls with frequency (boot 2 kHz 1.11 s, 4 kHz 0.80 s against the 1.8 dial). They need to point at the T_eff row (`ceiling_t`) instead, or test the dial path as proposed. 500 Hz and 1 kHz are +10.6 % and −12.7 % of the dial, inside ±15 %.

**Option 2. Fold ROOMTONE into the fit (fix A).**
- **What changes:** pass each line's `harm` stages into `design_bus` as part of `fixed` (alongside `pk`), physics path only, or both paths if you want the dial path to hit its dial in small rooms too.
- **Accuracy:** 3×4×2.5 125-500 Hz from −12 / −14 / −12 % to about +4 / +4 / 0 %. 2×2.5×2.2 250 Hz from −20 % to about +2 %.
- **Sound:** the octave average lengthens to the target. The ROOMTONE harmonics stay shorter than their neighbours, so the "room tone" colour is kept, but small rooms get about 15-25 % more low-mid tail than the B listens.
- **Risk:** physics-on numbers only if limited to phys. Phys-off goldens and MD5s are untouched. CPU zero (same section count). Hadamard untouched. A sections cache-key change.
- **Play and Render:** both get it automatically. The sections are designed in Python and shipped in the pack (`surf_sos`). Re-check the Play-Render null.

**Option 3. Stop the high-dial guard fallbacks (fix B).**
- **What changes:** in `design_line`, add a hard DC and sub-20 Hz constraint (for example a heavier penalty at the 0.01-20 Hz points, or limit the low-shelf gain so the loop at DC is ≤ the margin). On a guard failure, fall back to an S1 fit against T_eff with the kept cut excluded, not identity at the dial.
- **Accuracy:** expected 4 kHz at dial 4 / 8 from +10 / +15 % to about +4 %, and 500 Hz at dial 8 from −12 % to within about 5 %. This needs a measured prototype.
- **Sound:** a slightly darker, more physical top at long dials.
- **Risk:** physics-on dial 4 / 8 numbers only. Dial 1.8 is unchanged (no guard entries). CPU zero. Stability is the thing to prove: keep the existing guard as the last resort.
- **Play and Render:** both, via the pack.

**Option 4. 63 Hz (fix C).** Leave it. It is the noisiest band, shared with the dial path, and part of the axial-cut design. If wanted later, drop kept cuts above 30 Hz in the phys flat pack (as Surfaces mode does with D-SL3), at the cost of the existing axial-mode damping.

## Recommendation

Do **Option 1 now**: a measurement and notes fix, no audio change. That closes F9 and unblocks the physics default; your listening already approved the actual sound. Pair it with the question-9 test change (point the T60 checks at `ceiling_t` when physics is on). Queue **Option 3** next (small, phys-only, helps the dial 4 / 8 ambience), and **Option 2** as an Idle choice: it makes small rooms longer in the low mids, so it should be listened to before it is locked.
