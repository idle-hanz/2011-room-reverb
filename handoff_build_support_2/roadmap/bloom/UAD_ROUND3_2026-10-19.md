# UAD round 3: compressor hybrids, pre chains, aliasing, CPU (2026-10-19)

Data and scripts: `roadmap\bloom\uad\round3\` (`data\`, `scripts\`, `bench\`, `listen\`, `progress.md`).
Real music stayed on the PC. Only synthetic data went through the box. The real-music numbers come from `scripts\real_eval_pc.py`, which was run on the PC.

## 0. The short version (plain words)
- **The pres are the big win.**
  - **610-A:** the new model nulls 7–19 dB closer to the UAD than linear on all 10 held-out real-music rows. The median gain is 11.8 dB.
  - **Neve:** the new model is closer on all 10 rows too: median 1.6 dB, worst 0.1 dB, best 12.6 dB (vocal at drive 0).
  - Both pass their BLOOM_RUN ship rules. Neither uses the polynomial shaper that BLOOM_RUN prescribes, because those lose (see §3). That needs Idle's OK, and it's in the edit block.
- **Both UAD pres are really a sharp clipper at the drive point.** The Drive ±12 knob spans clean to hard clip. The "saturation" is mostly peaks hitting that ceiling.
- **Compressors.**
  - **Nuke:** the new dynamics are a clear win (real music −16.1 → −24.2 dB null, GR error halved).
  - **Room Crush:** better on every row (−2.1 dB median).
  - **Swell:** better on median (−1.7 dB). One drum row is 1.6 dB worse, which comes from the fixed EQ.
  - **Natural and Glue:** the new dynamics hit the step targets but are not better on music. Natural is 0.7 dB better on median but up to 4.6 dB worse on guitar/vocal. Glue is 2.0 dB better on median but up to 5.4 dB worse on the hot vocal.
- **Colour (the waveshapers)** now matches the UAD's H2/H3 levels and signs much better.
  - It shows up in IMD: the 1176 SMPTE goes from 0.7 % (current) to 5.3 % (proto), against 6.5 % on the UAD.
  - It moves whole-signal nulls by only ±0.5 dB, because the harmonics sit below the null floor. Judge it by ear.
- **EQ correction:** round 2's "−3.4 dB at 20 Hz HP" on the LA-2A, Distressor and SSL is a measuring artefact. The ESS kernel is flat (+0.2 dB at 20 Hz). Only the Fairchild has real LF loss, and only the 1176 and LA-2A have real HF tilt. Don't build the 20 Hz HPs.
- **Aliasing**, cheapest scheme that is ≥10 dB below the UAD or under −90 dBFS on every test row (two methods):
  - Glue: ADAA1
  - Nuke: ADAA1
  - Room Crush: none (1x)
  - Natural: 2x
  - Swell: 2x
  - 610-A: 8x
  - Neve: 16x (8x for the simpler Neve model)
- **CPU:** everything fits in the ~0.6 ms headroom (2.40 ms ceiling, cube about 1.79 ms). The 8x pre is about 0.11 ms per 128-sample stereo block native, about 0.14–0.21 ms in wasm (estimate).

## 1. Method (what was run)
- **Real excerpts (PC only, 6 s each, 44.1 → 48 kHz, peak 1):**
  - drums: Neil Zaza kick+snare+OH+hat
  - bass: NZ bass
  - guitar: NZ clean gtr
  - vocal: Emmavie main vocal
  - mix: Idle's "For Suno" mixdown
  - Each ran through every UAD preset at round 2 programme level (a) and +8 dB (b), and through the pres at drive 0 and −6.
- **Dynamics fits** (`fit.py`, Nelder–Mead, 4 restarts) target the round 2 A-list: static curve, step attack, and burst rel63/rel90.
- **Colour fits** (`colour_fit.py`) fit complex H2 and H3 (level and phase sign) on the UAD stairs at 100 Hz, 1 kHz and 5 kHz.
  - Each step uses the fitted GR.
  - Families tried per unit: poly (a2, a3), biased tanh, and each of those with a memory term (a one-pole LP on the distortion component), all with a GR depth law d0 + (1−d0)·clip(GR/Gs).
  - Room Crush also got a free-scale poly, which fixed the poly bound problem.
- **Fixed EQ** (`eq_fit2.py` and `eq_fit3.py`): a complex (magnitude + phase) fit to the UAD ESS kernel, 12 Hz–17 kHz. The 18–20 kHz UADx brickwall is excluded.
- **Pre fits** (`pre_fit.py`), Wiener–Hammerstein: an 8192-tap UAD linear kernel → shaper → kernel.
  - Families: poly3, poly5, btanh, softclip, and nclip (a smooth hard clip of order p0 with asymmetric knee p1, plus p2·u²), each also with emphasis, flux and memory variants.
  - Training data: stairs at 20/30/50/200 Hz and the r3 40/100/1k/5k stairs, plus ESS and noise.
  - Held out: noise at −6 and the synthetic vocal/mix/wetmix/drums programmes at 0 and −6.
  - Validated again on held-out real music.
- **Nulls** use the round 2 method (band-limited 20 Hz–19 kHz, LS gain). The round 2 drum floor is solved: it was out-of-band content plus a truncated 2048-tap kernel.
- **Aliasing**, three methods:
  - M1: Blackman-Harris peaks at the folded harmonic positions.
  - M2: time-domain LS removal of all true harmonics, ±60 Hz skirts excluded, then the peak below 16 kHz.
  - Sweep STFT, 2–20 kHz, as the relative check. Its absolute dBFS readings carry a constant −57.3 dB offset (double window normalisation), so read it only relatively.
  - M1 and M2 agree to within 1 dB on almost every row.
- **IMD:** SMPTE (60 Hz + 7 kHz) and CCIF (19 + 20 kHz) stairs. The SMPTE sidebands are also the gain-path test: 60 Hz drives the detector, 7 kHz is the HF probe.

- **So no order ≤3 polynomial passes for either pre.**

Shaper, per sample (unity small-signal slope): u = v/Vc, then d = S(u) − u with
- s = 1 + a when u > 0, 1 − a when u < 0, where a = p1 + mem_m·LP_τ(u)
- w = u/s
- S = s·w/(1+|w|^p0)^(1/p0) + p2·u²

Around it:
- Emphasis: a low-shelf LP1 into the shaper and its exact inverse after.
- Flux term: φ = LP1(v) at phi_fc, then c·Vφ·inverseLP((φ/Vφ)³).

Linear path: the UAD 8192-tap kernel. A low-order IIR fit is still to do, as BLOOM_RUN asks.

- **610-A nclip_flux:** Vc −16.77 dBFS (input-referred, at drive 0 = UAD mic gain such that −57 dBFS in is the round 2 1 % point), p0 12.57, p1 −0.1169, p2 −0.00776, phi_fc 199.8 Hz, phi_db −9.76, phi_c −0.0413.
  - Stairs (`pre_harm_check.csv`, drive −30..+6): THD within about 1.2 dB median at all four frequencies. H2/H3 within 1.3–2.8 dB.
  - θ2 is 37 / 68 / 88 / 90° against the UAD's 31 / 61 / 87 / 89° at 40 / 100 / 1k / 5k. That is the −x² tube followed by an output HPF, reproduced.
- **Neve nclip_emph_flux_mem:** Vc +9.19, p0 30 (at the bound: effectively a hard clip), p1 0.1193, p2 8e-5, emph 812 Hz −2.80 dB, phi 200 Hz (at the bound) +21.2 dB c 0.0529, mem_m 0.575, τ 18.9 ms.
  - Near clip it is good: THD 1.04 vs 1.20 % at 40 Hz drive 0, and 1.57 vs 1.58 % at 100 Hz.
  - It does not model the UAD's fixed odd floor (about −81 dBFS absolute, θ3 ≈ 180°), so below clip the THD reads 0.001 against 0.011 %. Not built: it is inaudible on a tail.
- **phi_fc sits at its 200 Hz bound in both fits.** The flux term acts as a gentle frequency-dependent cubic, not as an LF transformer.
- **Drive range (open sound question):** both UADs clip hard at the round 2 "1 % point".
  - Neve output ceiling about +12.7 dBFS: 0.005 % THD at −4, 1–15 % at 0, 20 % at +4.

- **Oversampling filter.**
  - Halfband FIR, 47 taps (24 non-zero side taps, Blackman): flat to −0.03 dB at 19.2 kHz, −34.5 dB at 27.8 kHz, −50.5 at 28.8, ≤−77.6 dB from 30 kHz. Images from 24–30 kHz fold to 18–24 kHz, above hearing.

  - An IIR allpass halfband (15 ns) would add phase shift, which costs nulls, so use the FIR.

Against the ceiling: worst case on top of the HANDOVER cube (about 1.79 ms; t19 about 1.62) is one comp at 2x plus a pre at 8x, about 0.12 ms native. Neve at 16x adds about 0.1–0.2 ms more: about 2.1–2.2 ms, still under 2.40, but tight. nclip uses two pow() calls; a pow-free p0 approximation would roughly halve its cost.

Colour, per sample:
- d = depth·vr·S(u) with u = v/vr, where depth = d0 + (1−d0)·clip(GR/Gs, 0, 1)
