# SL6 review, 2026-10-24

Verdict: keep Auto-treat shelved. Do not replace the seven octave `surf_cal` factors with third-octave factors from 50 to 500 Hz.

The long bass in the calibrated tail is those octave factors. A third-octave grid on the same one-pass law does not land 16/23 on this FDN. Holding the 125 and 250 Hz factors at 1 puts the bass back, and the formula on that line is still 14/23. No product file was edited. No impulse was rendered. The numbers below that are not from `SL6_ALGORITHM_AUDIT.md` are one `design_line` call, 30 ms, no kept cut, formula T against the Mäkivirta target.

Scene: accepted auto-treat rows on `real_default` (6 × 3 × 8 m, humidity 0.35, `t_mid` 0.8), `surf_directional` off. Porous 21% on F, D, A, C. Panels: membrane 62.5 Hz on F, membrane 125 Hz on D, Helmholtz 157.5 Hz on B, Helmholtz 198.4 Hz on D. Schroeder frequency 149.1 Hz. Model 20/24. The gate drops 40 Hz and scores 23 bands from 49.6 Hz to 8 kHz (`test_sl5_sl6_render.py`). On those 23 the model is inside ±10% on 20. It misses 99.2 Hz (1.139), 250 Hz (1.134), and 315 Hz (1.140).

## 1. The octave factors are the long bass

`calibration_factors` sets each factor to measured octave T20 / `target_T_oct`, clamped 0.6–1.6. `target_T_oct` is `room_target` at 125, 250, 500, 1 kHz, 2 kHz, 4 kHz, and 8 kHz. The gate scores the rendered third-octave T20 against Mäkivirta. `cal_fn` log-interpolates the seven factors onto the rate, so a factor below 1 lowers the loss and lengthens T. The calibrated pass used 0.829, 0.782, 0.952, 0.850, 0.878, 0.870, 0.906. VERIFIED in the audit.

Same line, formula T / Mäkivirta:

| | no factors | seven octave factors |
|---|---|---|
| third-octave fit error | 7.8% | 4.8% |
| bands inside ±10% | 21/23 | 7/23 |
| bass 50–500 Hz | 9/11 | 0/11 |
| 250 Hz | 1.045 | 1.439 |

The 4.8% means the fitter hit the lengthened target. It did not miss it. The bass formula with the factors sits at 1.25–1.44, in the same place as the audit's calibrated tail (1.18 at 63 Hz through 1.58 at 250 Hz). At 250 Hz the model is already 1.134 and the factor is 0.782, and 1.134 / 0.782 = 1.45. The fit returned 1.439. The rendered full IR at 250 Hz is 1.515. VERIFIED.

The factors-off render is the other half. Its 23 ratios, room average, are 0.845, 1.064, 1.239, 0.933, 0.806, 0.900, 0.835, 1.137, 1.020, 1.154, 1.083, 0.941, 0.854, 0.840, 0.843, 0.851, 0.838, 0.810, 0.741, 0.787, 0.825, 0.825, 0.852. Six land inside: 63, 99, 157, 315, 500, 630 Hz. That is the 6/23 in `test_sl6_auto_treat.py`. The octave meter then reads the whole octave short of `room_target`, one factor lengthens every third in that octave, and the five bass bands that were inside leave the window. From 630 Hz up, the same lengthening is what moves the short highs into the window, which is how 6/23 becomes 12/23. The bass excess and the high-band pass are one knob.

Checked against the other candidates:

- Fit resolution. S1P2 follows this smooth octave scale to 4.8%. The resolution limit shows up when the target wiggles per third, which is section 2. It is not why the calibrated bass is long.
- Flat fallback. The audit's L line 17 (655 samples, Z cut at 21.3 Hz) is the identity stamp that prints as 100. That section is 34% short at 50 Hz. One short line does not make a 32-line tail 20–58% long. The R twin, three samples longer, keeps its shelf. VERIFIED in the audit. This 30 ms line did not fall back.
- The 3× guard. On this line the deepest loop after the third-octave trial was −0.85 dB, and the guard line was −0.39 dB. The guard did not trip. The audit's trip is the short Z cut, not the rate scale.
- Modal density. `modes` counts 1182 modes at or under 500 Hz and 499 at or under 300 Hz (median gap 0.11 Hz, widest gap 8.65 Hz). The tail is long at 250 and 315 Hz, both above 149 Hz, and those two match the lengthened formula. The five gate bands at or under Schroeder are 49.6, 62.5, 78.7, 99.2, and 125 Hz. Sparse modes do not account for the excess above Schroeder.
- The Mäkivirta target. The model is the curve the loop is designed to, and it is inside the gate on 20 of 23 bands. Its three misses are +13 to +14%, smaller than the calibrated tail. Part of the 250 and 315 Hz misses is real: calibration chases `room_target`, and `room_target` is already outside Mäkivirta there. That is a few points, not the +45% the factor then adds.
- Scoring range. The fitter's third-octave error starts at 100 Hz (`THIRD` with the 100 Hz floor in `design_line`). The gate starts at 49.6 Hz. The 9.95% on R is line 27, under 10%, and it is an octave error as well as a third error. The 100 on L is the identity stamp. A clean line against the room rate, factors off, is 7.8%. The four panels are smoothed. They are not a 100% miss. "Thirds cannot follow, so 16/23 cannot pass" over-reads that stamp.

The 198 Hz hole in the full IR is a separate stage. The audit's calibrated tail there is 1.16. After early and direct it is 0.79. A rate factor does not fill a hole that appears when the direct sound is added.

## 2. Finer calibration does not reach 16/23

Third-octave factors from 50 to 500 Hz, same law (`T_measured / T_room`, clamp 0.6–1.6), octave factors kept above 500 Hz. One pass reflects the error: a third that rendered at 0.806 is asked for 1.48 times Mäkivirta. The arithmetic target, before the fit, is inside on 11 of 23 bands.

S1P2 then misses that wiggle. Third-octave fit error 19.1%, octave error 10.9%, formula inside 12/23, bass inside 4/11. The 125 Hz factor is 0.738 and the fit follows it: 125 Hz comes back at 1.470. The peak is spent there. 198 Hz was asked for 1.333 and came back at 1.081. Two peaks, Q at most 4, do not hold six bass corrections of alternating sign. The 3× guard still does not trip. VERIFIED on this line.

A half-step (square root of the bass factor) is inside on 12 of 23 before the fit. It was not fitted. It is already short of 16.

The rendered bass also scatters around the formula. Factors off, this line's formula is inside on 9 of 11 bass bands, and the render is inside on 5 of 11, with bands moving by as much as −25% (50 Hz) and +13% (79 Hz). That scatter is the width of the gate. Moving the knots does not remove it.

## 3. The simpler change still misses

The change that matches the cause is to leave the high octave factors and hold the 125 and 250 Hz knots at 1, on this room-average path only. Same line: fit error 4.4%, no guard, bass formula 1.03–1.16, 7 of 11 bass bands inside, 14/23 overall. The high shelf leaks downward. 315 Hz is 1.119, 397 Hz is 1.130, 500 Hz is 1.138, and several bands from 800 Hz to 1.6 kHz sit at 1.10–1.14. That is a formula on one line, not a rendered 16/23. The factors-off render already showed that a bass formula inside the window does not survive as a rendered third-octave T20.

Doing it means editing the calibration law (`cal_fn` / `surf_loop.py` or the auto-treat pass that builds `surf_cal`). That is an audio-core edit, so it is a Step 0 gate, not a wiring fix. It is not justified by a shown 16/23.

## 4. Polygon rooms stay on the shoebox

`auto_treat`, `room_target`, `face_areas`, `modes`, and `make_spec` take W, H, and L. None of them read `room_shape`. The Mäkivirta air term uses `W·H·L`. Panel frequencies come from shoebox axials (`surf_target.modes`, c = 343). The kept cuts use c = 340: W 28.3 Hz and L 21.3 Hz stay, H 56.7 Hz does not. `poly_room.py` keeps `n = (0, 0, 0)` on every tap and does not map faces onto A–F, so a directional hit list does not see the polygon. This proposal is the room-average path, which ignores hits and uses the shoebox rate on every line.

Splay, LEDE, live, gable, and hex change the delays and the early field (`compute_poly_taps`). When the fit hits, T = 60 / rate, so the designed tail length does not follow the new delays. The early pattern, the rendered Schroeder scatter, and the real mode frequencies do. A Box score, finer grid or not, is not a score for those shapes. The suggest step would still place the four panels on the shoebox axials.

## Left as they are

`RR_SL_STAGE` stays 5. `sl_room_result.js` stays `?v=5`. `POST /api/auto_treat` still only suggests. Do not edit `surf_loop.py`, the calibration clamps, or the Auto-treat button for this.
